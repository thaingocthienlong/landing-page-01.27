## Findings

### 1. HIGH - R-25: pale heading and promotion gradients fail contrast

`dist/styles.css:15-16,102-108,2653-2659,2995-2997` ends the heading and promotion text gradient at `#B8D5FB` or `#2E73CF`. The pale heading endpoint is about `1.51:1` against white and `1.40:1` against the mist background, below the `3:1` large-text minimum. The promotion endpoint is about `3.53:1` against the pale promotion surface while the label is small text. Keep the requested blue-to-light-blue identity, but darken the text endpoint or provide a contrast-safe text treatment.

### 2. HIGH - R-25: white and mint text crosses light gradient stops

`dist/styles.css:1616-1680,2693-2695,2764-2784` places consultation copy over a gradient ending at `#74B6FF`, and uses translucent white for body, schedule, and payment metadata. White on the light consultation endpoint is about `2.13:1`; the consultation mint accent `#A9F0EC` on the mid blue `#2D8BF2` is about `2.69:1`. Opacity makes the schedule and payment text weaker than their nominal white value. Darken the surface stops or move the copy onto solid contrast-safe blocks.

### 3. HIGH - R-03: scenario panel can overflow at the tablet transition

`dist/styles.css:880-886` keeps scenario tracks at `minmax(260px, ...) minmax(500px, ...)` until the `max-width:900px` collapse at `2211-2217`. Around a 920px viewport, the shell and gap leave less than the 824px minimum track width, so the panel can extend beyond its container before the mobile rule applies. Add a content-driven intermediate breakpoint or reduce the minimum workbench track.

### 4. HIGH - R-03: fixed contact rail has no page-wide mobile clearance

`dist/styles.css:2015-2022,2616-2629` fixes the contact rail to the lower-right corner. Mobile reserves right space for only selected regions at `2240-2246` and `2915-2918`; schedule, pricing, advantages, skills, and faculty content can still sit underneath the rail at 390px. Reserve a consistent safe lane, add safe-area handling, or use a non-overlapping mobile contact treatment.

### 5. HIGH - R-26/R-32/C-4: dialog fallback is not modal or Escape-dismissible

`dist/app.js:336-355` falls back to setting only the `open` attribute when `showModal()` is unavailable. There is no fallback Escape handler, focus trap/inert background, or fallback focus restoration, and the native `::backdrop` at `dist/styles.css:2825-2837` does not apply to a non-modal open dialog. `dist/index.html:346-381` also does not declare a fallback modal state. Make the fallback close on Escape, prevent background focus, restore the trigger focus, and provide an equivalent backdrop.

### 6. HIGH - R-26/R-32: adding and removing certificates can duplicate IDs

`dist/app.js:313-322` computes the next certificate index from the current row count. If row 1 is removed after row 2 is added, the next clone receives the existing row 2 IDs again. This breaks label association and the unique-ID contract. Generate the next index from the maximum existing suffix or use a stable unique counter.

### 7. MEDIUM - R-03: several mobile controls are below the 44px touch target

Mobile navigation links at `dist/styles.css:2185-2187` have no minimum height, the workbench buttons are 32px desktop and 34px mobile at `634-646` and `2367-2372`, and scenario tabs are about 42px at `858-866`. Increase the hit areas to at least 44px while preserving the smaller visual type scale.

### 8. MEDIUM - R-10: blur is used on three surfaces

`backdrop-filter` appears on the sticky header at `dist/styles.css:217-224`, the legacy consultation card at `1727-1741`, and the active consultation preview/modal surface at `2787-2795`. The antislop dose cap is one or two glass surfaces, and the unused card rule also indicates stale styling. Remove the inactive glass rule and keep blur on the one surface that needs it, or document the third use explicitly.

### 9. MEDIUM - C-2/R-15: conversion labels do not share the same behavior

The main navigation CTA and promotion link at `dist/index.html:18-22,40-47` point to `#consultation` and scroll to the section, while the consultation and closing CTA buttons at `335,341,388` open the popup through `data-open-consultation`. Decide whether `Đăng ký tư vấn` means “open the form” everywhere; if so, wire the links to the same behavior, or use a distinct label for a section jump.

### 10. MEDIUM - R-27: HTML and JavaScript phone grammars disagree

`dist/index.html:358` permits `+`, parentheses, periods, and repeated punctuation through its pattern, while `dist/app.js:377-383` strips those characters and accepts the result. The requested constraint is 9 to 11 digits after spaces or hyphens. Use one normalization grammar in both layers so punctuation cannot be accepted in arbitrary positions.

### 11. MEDIUM - R-36/C-5: internal implementation language is visible to learners

`dist/index.html:330` says “Thông báo nguồn”, and `349` tells visitors that fields were “đối chiếu với mẫu PHP”. The static limitation is honest, but PHP is an internal implementation detail and the wording is not natural audience copy. Say that the form follows the official notice and that online submission is not connected, without exposing the implementation source.

### 12. MEDIUM - C-3: the tuition note is marked up as code

`dist/index.html:213` wraps ordinary Vietnamese fee guidance in `<code class="section-command">`. This produces the annotated command-box treatment even though the sentence is explanatory prose. Use a normal paragraph or source note with normal reading semantics.

### 13. MEDIUM - R-32/R-34: focus indicators are too weak on light surfaces

The global focus ring at `dist/styles.css:2112-2115` is a 40% alpha blue that blends to roughly `#99BAF0` against white, around `1.97:1`. Form focus uses a 14% alpha shadow at `1805-1811`, around `1.25:1`; the border helps but the ring itself is not a reliable 3:1 non-text indicator. Use an opaque or dual-ring focus style that passes against both white and blue surfaces.

### 14. LOW - R-14: four advantage cards have no visual hierarchy

`dist/index.html:255-258` and `dist/styles.css:1484-1501` give all four advantages the same icon, padding, height, and card treatment. Equal weight may be intentional, but the audit found no reason in the design contract. Either promote the most important learning condition or record why equal treatment is the correct content hierarchy.

### 15. LOW - R-07: the technical dot texture repeats without a stated local purpose

The dot motif is used in the hero at `dist/styles.css:357-368`, the discount surface at `1282-1289`, and every advantage card at `1488-1510`. `DESIGN.md` explains the hero motif but does not explain the repeated discount and feature-card textures. Keep it where it communicates the analysis/workbench identity and remove or simplify the other repeats.

### 16. LOW - R-02: em dash characters remain in UI copy

`dist/index.html:91,201-203` uses U+2014 in `08—16.01.27`, `08—10`, `12—13`, and `14—16`. The copywriting rule bans em dashes in agent-authored UI text; the rest of the page already uses en dashes for ranges. Replace these with the same en-dash convention.

### 17. LOW - R-02/R-16: passive wording hides the actor

Examples include “hướng dẫn cài đặt sẽ được gửi” at `dist/index.html:255,301` and “Học phí được niêm yết” at `213`. These are source-backed statements, but “Viện sẽ gửi...” and “Thông báo số ... niêm yết...” make responsibility clearer without adding facts.

### 18. LOW - C-4/R-27: the form has no useful no-JavaScript fallback

`dist/index.html:350` uses `novalidate` without an action or method, while all behavior is installed in `dist/app.js:373-398`. If JavaScript fails, browser-native constraints are suppressed and the form has no useful next action. Keep the static boundary explicit with a no-JavaScript note, or allow native constraints to run when the enhancement is unavailable.

### 19. LOW - R-35: birth date accepts arbitrary text

`dist/index.html:357` uses `type="text"` with only a placeholder for the required birth date. It has no format pattern, input mode, or length check, so mobile users receive a generic keyboard and arbitrary strings pass until later review. If the PHP field must stay text, add explicit `dd/mm/yyyy` guidance and matching validation.
