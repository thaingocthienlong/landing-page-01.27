## Findings

### 1. HIGH - R-03/C-4: the narrow hero does not keep the primary action in the first screen

At the shared browser viewport, the local hero's title and supporting copy continue behind the fixed bottom rail; the `Đăng ký tư vấn` and `Chương trình chi tiết` buttons are below the visible first screen. The reference keeps its primary `Đăng ký tham gia` pill visible above the rail. The likely cause is the combined mobile minimum height and top padding at `dist/styles.css:3737-3767` (`min-height: max(650px/680px, ...)`, `padding: 138px/132px`), the large mobile title at `3764-3767`, and the long hero content at `dist/index.html:64-77`. The fixed rail is positioned at `dist/styles.css:2738-2808` and does not reserve content clearance.

Fix direction: compose a deliberate narrow hero state. Reduce the mobile top and bottom budget, constrain the H1 with a smaller fluid step, collapse the lead and schedule into one concise metadata row, and reserve the bottom rail lane so the primary CTA remains visible and tappable. Verify at 390px, 604px, 768px, and desktop widths, including landscape or short-height viewports.

### 2. HIGH - R-05/C-3: hero information hierarchy is denser than the reference

The reference hero has four visual beats: specialist badge, course title, launch-date line, and one action. The local hero adds a paragraph describing the three-course series, a separate date/location line, a second schedule/location paragraph, and two equal-weight actions (`dist/index.html:64-77`). The current CSS preserves those blocks at readable sizes (`dist/styles.css:3625-3655`) but the result makes the hero read like a course summary rather than the focused first-screen introduction shown by the reference.

Fix direction: keep the approved course title and one source-backed date/location line in the hero; move the longer three-course explanation into the registration or curriculum section; treat the registration CTA as primary and the curriculum link as a quieter secondary action. Preserve the existing facts and anchors while reducing first-screen copy density.

### 3. MEDIUM - R-05/R-26: top and bottom navigation chrome does not match the reference composition

The reference uses one floating glass top bar inside the hero, with a compact logo at left and a white pill registration control at right. The local first section stacks an announcement strip (`dist/index.html:18-22`), a larger wordmark header and multi-link navigation (`dist/index.html:24-52`), then a four-item bottom rail (`dist/index.html:54-61`). The local cascade intentionally styles the strip and header separately at `dist/styles.css:3426-3511`, while the bottom rail switches on at `2738-2776`. This creates more chrome and more navigation choices in the first viewport than the reference.

Fix direction: consolidate the first-screen chrome into a compact reference-like top bar, keep the announcement/deadline as a secondary line or move it below the hero, and reduce the mobile bottom rail to the three reference-level destinations. Preserve deep links (`#top`, `#registration`, `#programs`, and `#contact`) through the accessible menu rather than placing all of them in the first-screen rail.

### 4. MEDIUM - R-05/R-25: typography and CTA treatment still diverge from the reference language

The reference uses a smaller two-line uppercase display title with a white/cyan emphasis, a script-like cyan badge, and one white pill CTA. The local treatment uses the same general navy/cyan idea, but the title remains very large (`dist/styles.css:3608-3615`), the accent adds a pale-to-yellow gradient (`3608-3623`), and two pill buttons receive similar visual weight (`3662-3696`). At the shared narrow viewport this amplifies wrapping and pushes the action below the fold; at wider widths it makes the title and button group more dominant than the reference.

Fix direction: use a restrained two-line display scale for the hero, keep the accent endpoint contrast-safe on the navy surface, and make one white pill the clear primary action. Style the curriculum link as a lower-emphasis text/outline action with enough contrast and a 44px hit area.
